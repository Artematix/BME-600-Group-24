## Intro & Background (Mahlaqa, Laaraib)

The recall and the harm:
- FDA recall notice (Libre 3, late 2025). Use the FDA number: 860 injuries, 7 deaths.
- Kogai 2023, case report. False low reading, wrong treatment, DKA. Shows the harm path.
- FDA review of Libre Pro, 2016. FDA knew Libre read low back in 2016.

How accurate are CGMs normally:
- Vaughan 2025, meta-analysis. Ten years of Libre accuracy. Worse when low or changing fast.
- Libre in type 2 diabetes, 2022. 25,000 pairs. Libre read low in 75% of them.
- Freckmann 2023, review. Accuracy studies mostly cover adults. Many skip sex and ethnicity.
- Children study, Diabetes Care 2025. Dexcom reads slightly HIGH. Error direction depends on brand.

The gap:
- Mulley 2026. Closest to our work. Same dataset (REPLACE-BG), but only 2-week averages. We go further.
- Zhao 2023. Shanghai dataset paper. Noticed the sensor reads low, did not analyse it.

## Methods (Myleni, Artemy, Mirha)
- Kovatchev 2015. "Replay" method: real data + added error. Exactly what we do. Safe dosing below ~10% error.
- Facchinetti 2010. CGM error = lag + drift + noise. Why we test several fault types.
- Vettoretti 2015. Model of daily patient decisions under sensor error. Source of our treatment rules.
- Krouwer 2025. How to sort MAUDE CGM reports into cause categories. For Phase 1.
