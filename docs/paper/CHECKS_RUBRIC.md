### Do the Abstract, the end of the Introduction, the start of the Discussion and the Conclusion agree?

- The main numbers agree in all places: about 12 % too low for withheld corrections, 1.3 % too low on average in normal use, 69 to 77 % too low in reported failures. The end of the Introduction gives the question and no answer, and its 207,771 matches the Abstract.
- The answer itself does not agree. The Conclusion says "treatment decisions begin to change" at 12 %. The Abstract and the Discussion say this only for insulin corrections, and the Discussion says the display changes earlier (row 1).
- True lows not shown are "31.4 %" in the Abstract and the Discussion and "about 3 in 10" in the Conclusion. Use one form.
- The 69 to 77 % belongs to "a failure report" in the Abstract, to "the reports of deaths and injuries" in the Discussion and to "reported failures" in the Conclusion. The caption of Figure 2 says the 69 % also holds for malfunction-only reports. Use "reported failures" in all three.
- The advice has three versions (rows 4 and 5). The next step has two: four steps in the Discussion, three in the Conclusion.
